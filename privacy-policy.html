We adhere to strict data minimization principles. We collect only information that is relevant and necessary to fulfill your requested motorcycle repair, spare parts purchase, or doorstep pickup services.

If you request doorstep motorcycle pickup and delivery within Budaun, we collect:

This website does not use HTTP tracking cookies, marketing cookies, third-party advertising pixels, or web beacons. We do not track you across other websites, and we do not load third-party ad retargeting scripts.

When you submit a service booking inquiry through our website form, our script utilizes standard browser localStorage (key: lalla_bookings) solely on your own local device.

7. Data Security Safeguards

We implement technical and organizational safeguards to protect customer data:

  • HTTPS Encryption: All traffic between your browser and our website is protected using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.
  • End-to-End Chat Security: Direct customer messages sent via WhatsApp benefit from WhatsApp’s native end-to-end encryption protocols.
  • Access Restrictions: Customer contact details, pickup locations, and service records are accessible only to authorized workshop technicians and supervisory staff who require the information for vehicle repair and delivery.
  • No Online Financial Credential Storage: We do not process or store credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs on this website. Payments are made through standard in-person workshop settlement or direct bank/UPI channels.
